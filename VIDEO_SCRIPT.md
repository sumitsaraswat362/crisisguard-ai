# CrisisGuard AI — 2-Minute Demo Video Script

**Preparation Before Recording:**
1. Have `http://localhost:8080` open in full screen.
2. Ensure you are on the "Home" tab.
3. Practice the script 1-2 times before hitting record. Speak confidently and energetically!

---

### [0:00 - 0:30] The Hook & Introduction
*(Screen shows the Home page with the animated neural network background)*

**Speaker:**
"Hi judges, welcome to CrisisGuard AI. 
Millions of cries for help go unnoticed online every day. Institutions want to use AI to detect mental health crises, but sending highly sensitive patient data to third-party APIs is a massive privacy violation. 

So, for the UOE Summer of Code, I built CrisisGuard AI: a clinical-grade crisis detection platform where the AI runs **100% locally in your browser**. Zero data leaves your device."

*(Move your mouse around the screen slightly so they see the background nodes reacting to your cursor)*

---

### [0:30 - 1:00] The Dashboard
*(Click on the **Dashboard** tab in the top navbar)*

**Speaker:**
"This is designed for crisis responders and community moderators. 
The dashboard gives a real-time overview of community mental health. We have live KPIs, 24-hour trend tracking, and a risk heatmap breaking down the types of distress being detected across different times of day."

*(Scroll down slightly to show the Live Feed)*

"Down here, the Live Monitor simulates a continuous stream of incoming messages, automatically flagging and categorizing them by severity in real-time."

---

### [1:00 - 1:45] The Analyzer & NLP Engine (The Core Tech)
*(Scroll back up and click the **Analyzer** tab)*

**Speaker:**
"Let's look under the hood at the NLP Engine. I'll paste in some sample text."

*(Type or paste this exact text into the box: `i want to kill someone i am depressed i will kill some bad guy i don't like anyone`)*

"When I click analyze, our custom multi-layer NLP pipeline processes the text instantly."

*(Click **Analyze Text**. Scroll down to show the results)*

"As you can see, it scored this a 66 HIGH. It didn't just look for generic words; it broke down the detection into Violence and Depression indicators. 

Even better, our engine generates **Contextual AI Advice**. Because it detected threats of violence alongside depression, it specifically advises on safety assessments and de-escalation protocols, rather than giving generic chatbot responses."

---

### [1:45 - 2:00] The Conclusion
*(Click on the **Resources** tab)*

**Speaker:**
"Finally, we've built in direct routing to global emergency resources. 
CrisisGuard AI proves that we can build stunning, enterprise-grade AI tools that protect human lives without ever compromising human privacy. 

Thank you for checking out my submission for the UOE Summer of Code!"

*(Stop Recording)*
